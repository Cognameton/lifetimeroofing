Roofing FAQs

What areas does Lifetime Roofing & Services cover?

We serve homeowners within a 60-mile radius of Olive Branch, MS, including Southaven, Hernando, Collierville, Germantown, Bartlett, Lakeland, and the surrounding North Mississippi and West Tennessee communities.

Do you offer free roof inspections?

Yes. Every inspection is free and no-obligation. We document our findings with clear photos so you know exactly what's going on with your roof before you decide on anything.

Will homeowners insurance cover my roof damage?

Many storm-related roof issues, including hail, wind, and heavy rain damage, are covered by homeowners insurance. We help document the damage and work alongside your insurance company throughout the claims process.

How do I know if my roof has storm damage?

Storm damage isn't always visible from the ground. Missing or lifted shingles, granule loss, and small punctures can hide serious problems. A professional inspection after any major storm is the safest way to catch damage early.

What roofing services do you provide?

We handle roof repairs, full roof replacements, shingle and metal roofing, leak detection, and storm damage restoration for residential homes throughout our service area.

Can you repair a roof leak?

Yes. We trace roof leaks to their actual source — not just the visible water stain — and repair flashing, pipe boots, vents, missing shingles, and other problem areas. Call or text (901) 292-6207 for a free roof inspection.
