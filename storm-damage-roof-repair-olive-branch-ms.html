Hail & Wind Damage Repair

North Mississippi storms hit roofs in ways you can't see from the ground. Hail bruises shingles and knocks granules loose; high winds lift shingles and break their seal strips, opening the roof to the next rain. Lifetime Roofing & Services inspects, documents, and repairs storm damage for homeowners in Olive Branch, MS and the surrounding area before hidden damage turns into leaks and interior repairs.

  • Wind damage inspection & repair

Insurance Claims, Handled With You

Storm claims live or die on documentation and timing. We photograph every point of damage, provide the documentation your insurance company needs, and coordinate directly with your adjuster so nothing gets missed or minimized. You stay informed at every step — it's your claim and your roof.

Common Questions

FAQs

How do I know if my roof has storm damage?

From the ground you might see missing or lifted shingles, granules collecting in gutters, or dented vents and gutters — but hail bruising and broken shingle seals are usually invisible without getting on the roof. A free inspection after any significant hail or wind event is the only reliable way to know.

Will insurance cover my storm-damaged roof?

Hail and wind damage are covered by most homeowners policies. Timing matters — policies have claim deadlines. We document the damage thoroughly, meet with your adjuster, and help you understand what's covered through the whole process.

What if my roof is leaking right now after a storm?
