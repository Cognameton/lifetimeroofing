Roof Leak Repair

A water stain on the ceiling is usually a symptom, not the source. Leaks travel along decking and rafters before they show up inside, so patching the spot above the stain often misses the real entry point. We trace the leak to its actual source — flashing, valleys, penetrations, nail pops, or storm-damaged shingles — and repair it there, so it doesn't come back.

If your roof is leaking now, don't wait: prolonged water intrusion damages decking, insulation, and drywall, and can lead to mold. Call or text (901) 292-6207 and we'll get eyes on it.

Common Questions

FAQs

How much does roof repair cost in Olive Branch, MS?

It depends on the extent of the damage, which is why we start with a free inspection. Many repairs — a few missing shingles, a failed pipe boot, resealing flashing — are far less expensive than homeowners fear. We give you a clear price before any work starts.

Can you repair my roof, or will I need a full replacement?

Most of the roofs we look at can be repaired. We only recommend replacement when the roof is genuinely at the end of its life or repair costs would no longer make sense. Either way, you'll see photos of exactly what we found.

How quickly can you get to a roof repair?

For active leaks we prioritize scheduling, and after major storms we can tarp your roof to stop further damage while the repair is scheduled. Call or text (901) 292-6207 and we'll get you on the calendar.
