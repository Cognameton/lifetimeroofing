Free Roof Inspection in Olive Branch, MS

We get on the roof, document everything with photos, and give you an honest assessment — free, no obligation.

What a Free Inspection Includes

Every roof inspection Lifetime Roofing & Services performs in Olive Branch, MS is free and carries no obligation. We physically get on the roof — not a glance from the driveway — and check the components that actually fail:

  • Shingle condition: cracking, curling, bruising, granule loss
  • Flashing, valleys, pipe boots & vents
  • Signs of wind lift and hail impact
  • Gutters, drip edge & visible decking condition

You get clear photos of everything we find and an honest assessment of what actually needs attention — no guesswork, no pressure to book work you don't need.

When an Inspection Pays for Itself

Storm season in North Mississippi is the big one: hail bruising and wind-lifted shingles are often invisible from the ground, and insurance claims have time limits. If a storm has rolled through Olive Branch recently, an inspection now protects both your roof and your claim — see our storm damage repair page for what that process looks like.

Inspections are also smart before buying or selling a home, after your roof passes the 15-year mark, or any time you spot a ceiling stain. Catching a failed pipe boot early is a simple repair; catching it late can mean decking and drywall.

Common Questions

FAQs

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes — completely free and no-obligation. We inspect the roof, document what we find with photos, and explain it plainly. If nothing needs attention, we'll tell you that too.

When should I get my roof inspected?

After any major hail or wind storm, before buying or selling a home, if you see stains on interior ceilings, or if your roof is 15+ years old and hasn't been checked recently. Regular inspections catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

What do you check during an inspection?

Shingle condition, hail bruising and granule loss, flashing, pipe boots, vents, valleys, gutters, and visible decking issues — plus attic-side signs of leaks when accessible. Everything we find is photographed and walked through with you.
