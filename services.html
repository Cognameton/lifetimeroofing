Roofing Services

From a single repair to a full replacement, here's how we protect your roof.

Roof Repairs

Not every roofing problem calls for a full replacement. Our repair services target the specific issue, whether it's a handful of missing shingles, a damaged flashing, or a localized leak, so you're not paying for more than you need.

  • Shingle repair & replacement
  • Flashing & sealant repair
  • Ventilation repair
  • Minor storm damage patching

Full Roof Replacements

When a roof is beyond the point of repair, we handle the full tear-off and replacement, including shingle and metal roofing systems, so your home is protected for decades to come.

  • Complete tear-off & disposal
  • Asphalt shingle roofing
  • Metal roofing systems
  • Proper attic ventilation for roof longevity

Leak Detection

That small water spot on your ceiling is often a sign of a much bigger problem hiding above it. We track down the actual source of the leak, not just the visible symptom, to prevent structural damage, mold, and costly repairs down the road.

  • Interior & attic leak tracing
  • Flashing & penetration inspection
  • Documented findings with photos

Storm Damage Restoration

Hail, high winds, and heavy storms can damage a roof in ways that aren't visible from the ground. We inspect for bruised or cracked shingles, granule loss, lifted seals, and other hidden wind or hail damage, then restore your roof before small issues become major repairs.

  • Hail damage inspection & repair
  • Wind damage inspection & repair
  • Emergency tarping after major storms
  • Full photo documentation for your records

Free Roof Inspections

Every inspection we perform is free and no-obligation. We get on the roof, check for damage, and show you clear photos of exactly what's going on, no guesswork, no pressure to book work you don't need.

  • No-obligation, no-pressure visit
  • Detailed photo documentation
  • Honest assessment of what actually needs attention

Insurance Claim Assistance

Insurance companies expect quick action after storm damage, and delays can hurt your claim. We help document the damage thoroughly and work directly with your insurance company throughout the process, so you understand what's covered every step of the way.

  • Detailed damage documentation
  • Coordination with your insurance adjuster
  • Guidance through the claims process
