Roof Replacement in Olive Branch, MS

Complete tear-off and replacement with shingle or metal systems — built to protect your home for decades.

Full Tear-Off Roof Replacement

When a roof is beyond the point of sensible repair, Lifetime Roofing & Services handles the complete replacement for homeowners in Olive Branch, MS and surrounding communities. That means a full tear-off — not a layover — so we can inspect and address the decking underneath before the new system goes on.

  • Complete tear-off & haul-away of the old roof
  • Decking inspection and replacement of damaged boards
  • New underlayment, flashing, and drip edge
  • Proper attic ventilation for roof longevity

Shingle & Metal Roofing Systems

We install dimensional asphalt shingle roofs — the most popular choice in North Mississippi for their balance of cost, durability, and curb appeal — as well as metal roofing systems for homeowners who want maximum lifespan and storm resistance. Comparing the two? We'll lay out the numbers honestly so the decision is yours, not a sales script.

Recent storm damage? A replacement may be partly or fully covered — see how we help with storm damage and insurance claims.

Common Questions

FAQs

How do I know it's time to replace my roof?

Common signs include widespread granule loss, curling or cracking shingles, repeated leaks in different spots, sagging decking, and a roof past 20–25 years old. A free inspection with photos will show you exactly where your roof stands.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential replacements in the Olive Branch area are completed in one to two days, weather permitting. Larger or more complex roofs can take longer — we'll give you a clear timeline with your estimate.

Do you install metal roofs as well as shingles?

Yes. We install both dimensional asphalt shingle systems and metal roofing, and we'll walk you through the cost, look, and lifespan trade-offs so you can choose what fits your home and budget.

Get a free inspection first. We'll show you the roof's real condition and give you a straight answer on repair vs. replacement.
